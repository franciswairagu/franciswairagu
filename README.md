## 📂 Featured Projects

### 💼 JobPulse — Job Market Intelligence Platform

An end-to-end **job market intelligence and career recommendation platform** focused on helping job seekers understand the market and discover relevant opportunities.

The platform combines:

* Multi-source job data collection
* Data cleaning and normalization
* Job market analytics
* Machine learning
* NLP and semantic matching
* Skill extraction and normalization
* Career recommendations
* Skill-gap analysis
* AI-powered assistance

The long-term goal is to bridge the gap between **what employers are looking for and what job seekers currently have**, helping people understand not only which opportunities fit them, but also which skills they need to develop.
